Weeknight tomato soup (serves 4)

Ingredients: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, 2 cans whole tomatoes,
500 ml vegetable stock, 1 tsp sugar, salt, pepper, a handful of basil.

1. Warm the oil in a large pot and soften the chopped onion for 8 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and cook one more minute.
3. Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar. Simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth, season, and tear in the basil.
